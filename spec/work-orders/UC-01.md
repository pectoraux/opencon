## 2. Business objective

Prove a realistic consumer journey in which multiple consumers form privacy-preserving demand, qualified demand is exposed to suppliers, supplier competition produces a deterministic selection, fulfillment is executed through the existing lifecycle authority, realized savings are measured and evidence-qualified, and the resulting verified savings can fund a member benefit entitlement without creating a second economic authority.

The scenario is intentionally consumer-facing. It validates composition and product/API truth boundaries rather than merely replaying W036's business-procurement scenario.

## 3. Actors and tenancy

- A single community/consumer organization is the OpenCon tenant scope for the demand pool and benefit pool.
- Consumers are authenticated people. Their identities are server-resolved and their individual demand commitments remain private.
- The demand pool creator is the authenticated actor that creates the pool. No creator identity is caller-selectable.
- At least the frozen W024 privacy floor of active commitments participates. The test harness must explicitly prove the floor and threshold boundaries using the repository's frozen values rather than restating or lowering them.
- Suppliers act as authenticated people with ACTIVE membership in their supplier organization. Supplier eligibility is server-derived.
- Protected pool/selection/savings surfaces remain tenant- and role-scoped. Cross-tenant and nonexistent object identifiers fail closed without an existence oracle.
- Member benefit reads expose only the requesting member's own shares and totals.

## 4. Canonical executable path

The canonical path is one ordered composition:

```text
1. consumer DemandPool created in /demand
2. consumer commitments recorded with server-written aggregate-disclosure consent
3. qualified aggregate re-derived from current commitments at one explicit anchor
4. supplier offers recorded against the qualified aggregate
5. hard supplier eligibility derived before ranking
6. deterministic competitive selection committed
7. selected execution bound to an existing contribution/opportunity subject
8. sanctioned /workflows traversal through the measurement lifecycle point
9. real provider observation normalized by /measurement
10. authoritative /outcomes observation persisted with provenance/uncertainty
11. supported W027 baseline/counterfactual resolved
12. savings derived and verified through /demand + /evidence + /outcomes contracts
13. W028 Benefit Pool created with `verified_savings` funding reference
14. deterministic entitlement allocation recorded by /benefits
15. member reads own allocation through the existing privacy-preserving member view
16. final lineage reconstructed from authoritative records and durable audit order
```

The implementation must not invent an intermediate procurement state machine, local lifecycle, alternative evidence authority, savings arithmetic outside W027, benefit ledger, payment authority, client authority, or new domain.

## 6. Economic contract

UC-01 deliberately distinguishes **verified savings** from **economic settlement**.

```text
consumer/procurement activity
→ measured fulfillment outcome
→ evidence-backed W027 savings decision
→ authoritative `verified_savings` funding reference
→ W028 deterministic entitlement allocation
```

A savings-funded W028 allocation is entitlement-only. It does not mint cash, credits, ledger balances or payment obligations. The implementation must prove that no economic record is created outside the existing `/settlement` authority.

Any requirement for cash redemption, external payment execution, partial economic draw of savings, or a new member redemption ledger is a capability gap and is explicitly outside UC-01.

## 7. Trust and failure contract

The implementation must cover the following material properties:

1. server-side authorization at every protected command;
2. tenant scoping with cross-tenant/nonexistent parity;
3. privacy floor and aggregate-only disclosure;
4. hard supplier eligibility before ranking;
5. deterministic selection;
6. workflow authority with sanctioned transitions only;
7. provider adapter isolation and secret-provider fail-closed behavior;
8. evidence/outcome provenance and uncertainty preservation;
9. same-key idempotent replay before mutable reads where required;
10. real concurrent race(s) for commitment/selection/allocation invariants;
11. transaction-level fault injection after material work is staged for the critical composite mutation, followed by a healthy same-key retry;
12. post-commit audit publication and rollback discard;
13. deterministic fixed-anchor proof fixtures;
14. targeted mutation checks with byte-identical restoration;
15. repository-wide architecture and authority regression.

## 8. Provider contract

At least one realistic provider-backed measurement round-trip is required because measurement is an existing capability and provider semantics must remain behind the existing adapter boundary.

Provider-specific SDK/types or raw vendor payloads must not cross into `/outcomes`, `/demand`, `/benefits`, `/workflows`, `/evidence`, or any other domain authority.

No new payment provider integration is authorized by UC-01. The existing payment/settlement boundaries are observed only to prove that savings-funded entitlement does not silently become a second economic system.

## 9. Product/API contract

The member outcome is product-relevant, but the protocol repository remains authoritative.

If the current `/api` boundary cannot expose the required consumer journey or member-benefit read model, implementation must stop at that boundary and record an API/product capability gap. A client-side reconstruction of demand, savings, eligibility, allocation or lifecycle truth is prohibited.

The production browser session/auth wiring and other UX-01 follow-on gaps remain separately scoped unless the architect authorizes them as a distinct work item.

## 10. Evidence contract

The implementation evidence ledger must map every UC-01 acceptance criterion to:

- exact changed files;

- command output and counts;
- authoritative state/version witnesses;
- durable audit-order reconstruction;
- real PostgreSQL and Redis integration;

## 11. Acceptance criteria

### UC-01-AC-01 — Demand authority
Consumer demand pools and commitments use the existing `/demand` commands, current-state derivation, privacy floor and server-resolved actors. No second demand/procurement authority exists.

### UC-01-AC-02 — Privacy and qualification
Qualified aggregates are derived from authoritative records at a fixed anchor; suppressed groups remain suppressed; individual consumer terms, ids and exact commercial data do not cross the supplier surface.

### UC-01-AC-03 — Supplier competition
Supplier offers use existing `/demand` semantics. At least one hard-ineligible supplier is rejected before ranking. The committed winner is deterministic and reproducible.

### UC-01-AC-04 — Fulfillment lifecycle
The selected execution traverses the existing `/workflows` lifecycle to the required measurement/verification states. No local lifecycle engine or direct state mutation appears.

### UC-01-AC-05 — Measurement and savings evidence
A real provider observation is normalized through the measurement boundary into `/outcomes`; provenance and uncertainty are retained; W027 rejects unsupported/stale/invalid savings evidence.

### UC-01-AC-06 — Benefit entitlement
W028 consumes an authoritative `verified_savings` reference and records a deterministic, conservation-bounded entitlement allocation. No second ledger or caller-supplied funding amount exists.

### UC-01-AC-07 — Member privacy surface
The member benefit view exposes only the requesting member's own entitlement facts and does not reveal another member's identity, weight, amount or protected procurement data.

### UC-01-AC-08 — End-to-end lineage
The complete journey is proven in executable order using authoritative record/state-version witnesses plus durable audit commit ordering.

### UC-01-AC-09 — Trust, atomicity and recovery
Replay, concurrency, transaction-fault rollback/retry, tenancy, authorization, secret isolation, privacy and audit ordering are proven with behavioral tests and targeted mutations where material.

### UC-01-AC-10 — Architecture/product boundary
Frozen architecture remains byte-identical, no new dependency edge/domain/ledger/payment authority is introduced, and any missing API/product/provider capability is classified rather than implemented opportunistically.
