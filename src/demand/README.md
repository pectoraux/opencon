# `demand` boundary

**Tier:** domain
**Authority:** demand aggregation
**Architecture ref:** `spec/architecture.md` §18 (Module ownership), §9 (Demand architecture)
**Concrete behaviour:** NET-W024 — consumer demand pools; NET-W025 — business procurement pools; NET-W026 — supplier offers and competitive selection; NET-W027 — verified savings and counterfactuals

## Scope

`/demand` owns the consumer demand-pool domain (NET-W024; requirements DEM-001..003), the business procurement-pool domain (NET-W025; requirements DEM-001..003 + PROC-001..003 bounded to demand/qualification/supplier-discovery) AND the supplier-offer/competitive-selection domain (NET-W026; requirements DEM-003 + PROC-001/PROC-003 bounded to offer/selection) AND the verified-savings/counterfactual domain (NET-W027; requirements PROC-002 + PROC-AC-01's gate + OUT-004/EVID-005 alignment) — the SAME frozen boundary, NOT a second demand, procurement or selection authority:

- **Pools** — first-class, tenant-scoped, durable records with an explicit, versioned qualification policy and one-way closure. The acting person at creation is the creator (no creator input exists). Procurement pools add the distinct-organization threshold (the competition-policy dimension of the qualification policy).
- **Commitments** — first-class, tenant-scoped, durable, PRIVATE records with bounded provider-neutral attributes (closed region/quantity/budget-band vocabularies in `src/core/demand.ts`; procurement categories, business-scale budget bands, unit-price bands and delivery-timing windows in `src/core/procurement.ts`), a server-written aggregate-disclosure consent grant, and one-way withdrawal. The acting person at submission is the consumer/submitter (no consumer or submittedBy input exists). One active commitment per (pool, consumer) — and per (pool, buyer organization) for procurement, where the acting person must additionally hold ACTIVE membership in the named buyer organization (server-enforced, indistinguishable from a nonexistent organization).
- **The derived qualified aggregate** — the privacy/competition-preserving supplier-facing view (`aggregation-engine.ts` / `procurement-aggregation-engine.ts`): re-derived from CURRENT durable records on every evaluation at ONE explicit anchor; deterministic digest over the decision facts; aggregate facts (including the counts) emitted only above the frozen privacy floor (W024) — or above the frozen commitment floor AND the frozen distinct-organization floor (W025, PROC-003); below-floor distribution groups suppressed (counted, never named). Never stored, never caller-asserted. Exact quantities, unit prices, budgets and timing never cross — only bands/buckets/windows.
- **Supplier offers** — first-class, tenant-scoped, durable, PRIVATE records with bounded provider-neutral attributes from the SAME closed vocabularies (`src/core/procurement-offer.ts`), a server-written competitive-selection consent grant, an explicit validity window and one-way withdrawal (`procurement_offers`). The acting person at submission is the supplier (no supplierPersonId input exists) and must hold ACTIVE tenant membership; the pool must be currently QUALIFIED (re-derived server-side pre-flight AND in-tx; an unqualified or closed pool fails closed). One active offer per (pool, supplier).
- **Competitive selection** — the derived selection view (`competitive-selection-engine.ts`: server-derived hard eligibility — pool qualification, offer validity, named-region service, supplier authorization — then the explicit versioned ranking policy with the stable offer-id tie-break, deterministic digest excluding the anchor) plus the AUTHORITATIVE selection lineage records (`procurement_selections`, immutable, PROC-AC-03: the selection records the offer set and the selection rationale). Selection surfaces are pool-creator-only (supplier commercial terms never cross to other pool participants).
- **Verified savings & counterfactuals** — the explicit `ProcurementBaseline` records (kind `baseline`/`counterfactual` — the NET-W006 BaselineKind reused; closed method vocabulary + required method version; bounded historical comparison window; quantified confidence with the counterfactual-interval rule; measurement provenance; ≥1 subject-bound `/evidence` references; one-way invalidation) plus the derived savings view (`savings-engine.ts`: twelve machine-readable sufficiency checks — baseline validity/interval/evidence support/freshness, observation presence/support/chain-head/subject/outcome-type/freshness, unit consistency, uncertainty preservation; conservative combination; server-owned `baseline − observed` arithmetic; deterministic digest excluding the anchor) and the AUTHORITATIVE savings lineage records (`procurement_savings`, immutable — recording FAILS CLOSED on unsupported derivations). Realized-outcome facts are `/outcomes` observations (outcome type `savings`, subject `(procurement_pool, poolId)`) consumed through NEUTRAL composition-root lookups; the W026 selection reference is neutral lineage only. All savings surfaces are pool-creator-only. Economically authoritative consumers (NET-W028+) must consume the DERIVED evaluation, never stale snapshots.

## Authority separation

- `/settlement` remains the sole economic authority: NO ledger entries, credits, cash, stakes or rewards are created here — commitments mint nothing, SELECTIONS MINT NOTHING (a selection is a procurement decision, never an economic one) and VERIFIED SAVINGS CLAIMS MINT NOTHING (a savings claim is a measurement decision; savings never settle by themselves — economically authoritative use requires the future `/settlement`-side gate).
- `/identity`, `/organizations`, `/participants` remain the membership/authorization authorities: membership resolves read-only through the neutral `DemandMembershipLookup` (wired at the bootstrap root over the organizations membership repository); consent and membership are server-enforced, never client-asserted. The authorized-supplier gate is ACTIVE tenant membership (server-resolved).
- `/workflows` is untouched: closure, withdrawal, offer withdrawal and baseline invalidation are one-way field mutations, not status machines; offer expiry, evidence staleness and observation supersession are DERIVED at the evaluation anchor, never mutated.
- No coupling to `/reputation`, `/disputes`, `/campaigns`, `/inventory` or `/adapters` — qualification, eligibility, ranking and savings cannot be influenced by activity, spend, wealth or reputation. The ONLY cross-boundary reads are the NET-W027 NEUTRAL lookups over `/evidence` and `/outcomes` (read-only facts: scope, subject binding, source type, observed value/confidence/provenance/chain position — declared as structural interfaces in this port and wired at the bootstrap composition root; measurement semantics stay in `/outcomes`, provenance/truth stays in `/evidence`). No AI path exists anywhere in this surface (any future advisory signal may only blend in AFTER deterministic sufficiency, and can never authorize a savings claim).

## Conventions

Material mutations follow the NET-W003/004/008/019/020 conventions: composite idempotency keys, per-pool advisory locking, ONE authoritative transaction, `...WithinTx` repository twins, transactional audit buffers (`demand_pool.created` / `demand_pool.closed` / `demand_commitment.recorded` / `demand_commitment.withdrawn` / `procurement_pool.created` / `procurement_pool.closed` / `procurement_commitment.recorded` / `procurement_commitment.withdrawn` / `procurement_offer.recorded` / `procurement_offer.withdrawn` / `procurement_selection.recorded` / `procurement_baseline.created` / `procurement_baseline.invalidated` / `procurement_savings.recorded`). The derived evaluations mutate and audit nothing.

## Files

- `port.ts` — the declared public interface (records, inputs, derived views, lookups, repositories, services).
- `module.ts` — boundary registration.
- `authority-demand-repositories.ts` — PostgresAuthority-backed append-only repositories (`demand_pools`, `demand_commitments`).
- `aggregation-engine.ts` — the pure deterministic privacy-preserving derivation + canonical digest (W024).
- `demand-service.ts` — the consumer demand domain service (commands + derived evaluation).
- `authority-procurement-repositories.ts` — PostgresAuthority-backed append-only repositories (`procurement_pools`, `procurement_commitments`) (W025).
- `procurement-aggregation-engine.ts` — the pure deterministic privacy/competition-preserving derivation with the dual frozen floors (W025).
- `procurement-pool-service.ts` — the business procurement domain service (commands + derived evaluation) (W025).
- `authority-supplier-offer-repositories.ts` — PostgresAuthority-backed append-only repositories (`procurement_offers`, `procurement_selections`) (W026).
- `competitive-selection-engine.ts` — the pure deterministic hard-eligibility + ranking derivation (W026).
- `supplier-offer-service.ts` — the supplier-offer/competitive-selection domain service (material commands + derived evaluation) (W026).
- (vocabulary) `../core/procurement-savings.ts` — the closed savings/counterfactual vocabulary, frozen bounds, derivation policy, validators and errors (W027).
- `authority-savings-repositories.ts` — PostgresAuthority-backed append-only repositories (`procurement_baselines` with one-way invalidation, `procurement_savings` immutable lineage) (W027).
- `savings-engine.ts` — the pure deterministic, uncertainty-preserving savings derivation + canonical digest (W027).
- `savings-service.ts` — the savings/counterfactual domain service (material commands + derived evaluation over neutral /evidence + /outcomes lookups) (W027).
